Privacy

  • Notes and screenshots are stored in this browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent anywhere by default.
  • The extension does nothing on a page until you start a review on that tab.
  • No analytics, no remote code. Screenshots may contain sensitive page content — export and sync are explicit actions.
